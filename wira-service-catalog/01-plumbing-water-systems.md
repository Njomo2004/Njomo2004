# Trade 1: Plumbing & Water Systems (Kenya)

## Trade overview (Layer 1)
Scope includes potable water supply (PPR, copper, galvanized), drainage (PVC/cast iron), fixtures, borehole + tank systems, booster pumps, and gravity‑fed installations typical in Kenya. Excludes major civil works (large excavation), full MEP design, and HVAC piping.
