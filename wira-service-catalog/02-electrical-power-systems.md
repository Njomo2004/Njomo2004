# Trade 2: Electrical & Power Systems (Kenya, 240V single‑phase)

## Trade overview (Layer 1)
Covers residential/light‑commercial electrical work: wiring repairs, sockets, lighting, distribution boards, breakers, earthing, generators/inverters, and small appliances wired into mains. Excludes heavy industrial 3‑phase design and utility‑side work.
